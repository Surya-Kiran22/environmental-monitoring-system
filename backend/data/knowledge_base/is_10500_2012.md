# IS 10500:2012 Drinking Water - Specification (Second Revision)

Issuer: Bureau of Indian Standards. Sample transcription for demonstration.

## Table 1 - Turbidity, permissible limit
Turbidity, NTU: acceptable limit 1; permissible limit in the absence of alternate source 5. Method: IS 3025 (Part 10).

## Table 1 - Total dissolved solids, permissible limit
Total dissolved solids, mg/l: acceptable limit 500; permissible limit in the absence of alternate source 2000.

## Table 1 - pH
pH value: 6.5 to 8.5, no relaxation.

## Use in this system
IS 10500 applies to drinking water. In this deployment the turbidity and TDS limits are configured as screening benchmarks for raw source water. Findings against them are labelled screening comparisons, not legal exceedances.
