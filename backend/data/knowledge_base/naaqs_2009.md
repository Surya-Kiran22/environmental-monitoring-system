# National Ambient Air Quality Standards (NAAQS) 2009

Issuer: Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB), India. Notified November 2009 (G.S.R. 826(E)).
Sample transcription for demonstration. Verify against the official gazette before regulatory use.

## Applicability
The standards apply to industrial, residential, rural and other areas. Separate annual limits for SO2 and NO2 apply to ecologically sensitive areas notified by the Central Government. Concentrations are referenced to ambient conditions.

## Schedule, item 1 - SO2
Sulphur dioxide (SO2). Annual average: 50 µg/m³. 24-hour average: 80 µg/m³. Methods: improved West and Gaeke; ultraviolet fluorescence.

## Schedule, item 2 - NO2
Nitrogen dioxide (NO2). Annual average: 40 µg/m³. 24-hour average: 80 µg/m³. Methods: modified Jacob and Hochheiser (Na-arsenite); chemiluminescence.

## Schedule, item 3 - PM10
Particulate matter (size less than 10 µm), PM10. Annual average: 60 µg/m³. 24-hour average: 100 µg/m³. Methods: gravimetric; TEOM; beta attenuation.

## Schedule, item 4 - PM2.5
Particulate matter (size less than 2.5 µm), PM2.5. Annual average: 40 µg/m³. 24-hour average: 60 µg/m³. Methods: gravimetric; TEOM; beta attenuation.

## Schedule, item 5 - Ozone
Ozone (O3). 8-hour average: 100 µg/m³. 1-hour average: 180 µg/m³. Methods: UV photometric; chemiluminescence; chemical method.

## Schedule, item 7 - CO
Carbon monoxide (CO). 8-hour average: 2 mg/m³. 1-hour average: 4 mg/m³. Method: non-dispersive infrared (NDIR) spectroscopy.

## Averaging and compliance notes
Annual arithmetic mean is computed from a minimum of 104 measurements in a year, at a particular site, taken twice a week 24-hourly at uniform intervals. 24-hourly, 8-hourly or 1-hourly monitored values, as applicable, shall be complied with 98 % of the time in a year; 2 % of the time they may exceed the limits but not on two consecutive days of monitoring.
