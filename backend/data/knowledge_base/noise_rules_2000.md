# Noise Pollution (Regulation and Control) Rules, 2000

Issuer: Ministry of Environment and Forests, India (S.O. 123(E), 2000, as amended). Sample transcription for demonstration.

## Schedule - Ambient air quality standards in respect of noise
Limits are expressed in dB(A) Leq.

Category A - Industrial area: day time 75 dB(A), night time 70 dB(A).
Category B - Commercial area: day time 65 dB(A), night time 55 dB(A).
Category C - Residential area: day time 55 dB(A), night time 45 dB(A).
Category D - Silence zone: day time 50 dB(A), night time 40 dB(A).

## Definitions
Day time shall mean from 6.00 a.m. to 10.00 p.m. Night time shall mean from 10.00 p.m. to 6.00 a.m.
Silence zone is an area comprising not less than 100 metres around hospitals, educational institutions, courts, religious places or any other area declared as such by the competent authority.
dB(A) Leq denotes the time-weighted average of the level of sound in decibels on scale A, which is relatable to human hearing.

## Computation note used by this system
Equivalent continuous level over a period is computed as Leq = 10 log10( (1/n) sum 10^(Li/10) ) over the hourly LAeq values inside the day or night window. A period is assessed only when at least 75 % of its hours are available.
