# Sensor Manuals - Summary Notes

## Beta attenuation PM monitor (PM2.5 / PM10)
Typical range 0-1000 µg/m³. High humidity (above 85 %) can inflate readings on optical sensors; beta attenuation monitors with heated inlets are less affected. Sudden step changes with no co-movement in PM10 suggest a tape or flow fault.

## NDIR carbon monoxide analyser
Range 0-50 mg/m³. Single-hour spikes above 20 mg/m³ that revert within one hour, without a matching rise in NO2 or PM, are characteristic of electrical interference or zero drift. Recommended action: zero/span check before using the data.

## Optical turbidity probe
Range 0-1000 NTU. Bio-fouling and air bubbles produce noisy upward drift; a sustained multi-hour rise that co-occurs with changes in conductivity or dissolved oxygen is more consistent with a real change in water quality. Recommended action: wipe and verify against a grab sample.

## Class 1 sound level meter
Range 30-130 dB(A). Wind above 5 m/s may bias LAeq upward without a windscreen.
